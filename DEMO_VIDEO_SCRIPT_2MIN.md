## 💡 Pro-Tips for Recording & Editing

1. **Screen Resolution:** Record all browser demos at **1920×1080 (16:9)** at 100% or 110% browser zoom for maximum readability.
2. **Audio Quality:** Use a clean lapel mic or headset; keep background music low (ducking audio at -22 dB during speech).
3. **Cursor Highlights:** Enable a subtle yellow highlight around the mouse pointer so clicks are immediately visible.
4. **Smooth Transitions:** Use 0.3s quick cross-dissolve or slide transitions between member handovers to maintain high pacing.
